Bubble Pop MasterPrivacy Policy

✉︎ Business@joemet123.com

Effective 13 September 2026 · Published by Joseph Maglione

Bubble Pop Master is a bubble-popping game for very young children on iPhone and iPad. It does not collect any data about you or your child. None at all.

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software of any kind. It makes no network requests except to Apple, which handles the one optional in-app purchase. Your settings and bubble count stay on your device. The rest of this page says the same thing in more detail.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or any other support request: Business@joemet123.com. Put "Bubble Pop Master" in the subject and you will have a reply within 30 days. Joseph Maglione publishes the app and is responsible for it.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it has no way to send us anything.

What leaves the device

Nothing. Bubble Pop Master makes no network requests of its own. The one exception is Apple's App Store, which handles the single optional in-app purchase (a one-time $1.99 unlock) and the restoring of a previous purchase. Those transactions are between you and Apple: we never see your payment details, and no personal information reaches us as part of them. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What is stored on the device

The app remembers your settings and a running count of bubbles popped: the chosen background, bubble style and number of bubbles, whether sound and haptics are on, which play mode is selected, the total pops and your best round. All of it is kept on the device itself, in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Bubble Pop Master is made for children and is offered in Apple's Kids Category. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category requirements. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, there is nothing to disclose, nothing to share, and nothing for a parent to request or delete beyond removing the app.

The app contains no advertising, no links to websites, no social media features and no way to communicate with anyone. The one in-app purchase sits behind a parental gate that a young child is not expected to be able to pass.

Tracking

Bubble Pop Master does not track users, does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and does not share any data with data brokers or advertising networks. It will never show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing it would ask permission for.

Motion

An optional "Tilt To Steer" setting, off by default, reads the device's motion sensors (its gravity reading, with the accelerometer as a fallback) so the bubbles drift the way the device is leaning. Those readings move bubbles on screen in that instant and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Hosting

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may log ordinary connection data such as your IP address, as any web host does. That is described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. The app itself never opens this page or any other website.

Changes

If the app ever begins collecting anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date, and the change will be described here rather than quietly absorbed.
