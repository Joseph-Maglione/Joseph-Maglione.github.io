Bubble Pop Master is made by one person, and that person reads every email. Whatever it is, write in and you will have a reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Email:

Business@joemet123.com

Put "Bubble Pop Master" in the subject so it is not missed, and say which iPhone or iPad you are using and which version of iOS. There is no phone line, no live chat, and no social media account for the app; email is the one way in.

Purchases and restoring

The one-time "Unlock Everything" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. To bring it back on a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings in the app, pass the grown-up gate, and tap "Restore A Previous Purchase". It is shared with everyone in your Family Sharing group. For a refund, ask Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot see or reverse payments ourselves.

Bugs and ideas

If something looks wrong, sounds wrong, or a bubble will not pop, say what you did just before, and attach a screenshot or screen recording if you can. Requests for new backgrounds, bubble themes and sounds are welcome too; several of the ones in the app started as emails.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and sends nothing, so there is no account to look up and no data to delete. Everything about that is on the privacy policy page. Questions about it go to the same address.
